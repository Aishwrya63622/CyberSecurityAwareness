Learn about phishing, digital arrest scams, and malicious applications.

Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Phishing is a scam where criminals send fake messages or websites to steal passwords, banking information or personal data.

🚨 Digital Arrest Scam

Scammers may pretend to be police or government officials and threaten people with fake legal cases.

📱 Malicious Applications

Some harmful applications can steal personal data, passwords or other sensitive information.

🛡️ Important Safety Tips

  • Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • Do not click unknown or suspicious links.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.
  • Download apps only from trusted sources.
  • Keep your phone and computer updated.
  • Report suspicious cybercrime incidents to the appropriate authorities.
